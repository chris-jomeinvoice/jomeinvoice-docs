# Share your e-invoice request link and QR code

*3 min read · B2C businesses*

The Request module lets your B2C customers request an individual e-invoice within 72 hours of their purchase — by scanning a QR code or following a link. You don't need to create the invoice manually; the customer fills in their own details and you approve.

## How it works

1. **Get your request link and QR code**

   Go to **Settings → Workspace Settings → Share**. JomeInvoice provides a unique request URL and QR code for your workspace.

   *[Screenshot: Settings → Workspace Settings → Share — QR code and shareable link]*
2. **Display or share it at point of sale**

   Options for sharing:

   - **Print the QR code** — place it at your cashier counter, on receipts, or at the table
   - **Share the link** — include it on your website, in emails, or in messaging

   When a customer scans or clicks, they land on a JomeInvoice-hosted form to enter their TIN, name, and address.
3. **Customer submits their request**

   The customer fills in their details. JomeInvoice validates their TIN against LHDN in real time — so by the time the request reaches you, the TIN is already verified.
4. **You approve and the e-invoice is issued**

   Incoming requests appear under **Request → Pending**. Review the customer details and click **Approve**. JomeInvoice converts the request into a valid e-invoice and submits it to LHDN.

   The approved invoice is automatically excluded from monthly consolidation — so it won't be counted twice.

   *[Screenshot: Request module — Pending requests list with Approve action]*

> [!WARNING] 
> The 72-hour window for buyer requests starts from the time of the original purchase — not from when they submit the request. If they request after 72 hours, the request will be rejected.
